# TestFlight

Copy for the TestFlight external testing group (family testers). Build 2026102901, version 1.

## Beta App Description

Patchwork Valley is a calm puzzle game about stitching a lost valley back together, one six-sided patch at a time. Match each patch's sides to its neighbors' meadows, forests, fields, villages and rivers, finish quests to earn rewards, and spend them on helpful power-ups. A short guided start shows you how to play, so just open it and follow along. There are no ads.

## What to Test

- Play the first valley from the very start and follow the coach. Tell us anywhere it felt confusing, too slow or too fast.
- Keep going through valleys 2 to 5. Each one brings a new power-up with a free try; tell us whether each one makes sense.
- Listen for the soft cloth tap when you choose a spot (sound follows the silent switch). Does it feel nice, or does it get annoying?
- Finish a valley, tap View valley to lift the fog, and save it to the Gallery.
- Try Levels and Zen from the menu at the top left. The menu's Debug section is a testing tool, so please skip it.
- When something looks wrong or you get stuck, take a screenshot and send it with Share Beta Feedback.
